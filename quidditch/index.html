How to Play

STEERDrag on the left half. Your broom flies forward on its own. Double-tap and hold to air-brake into tight turns.
BOOSTHold the bolt. Tuck into another flyer's slipstream to refill it fast.
PASS & SHOOTPASS throws to the teammate nearest your aim; without the ball it calls for a pass. Hold SHOOT and release in the green part of the ring for a perfect shot the Keeper struggles to stop. The hoop marker turns green when you're lined up.
DODGESwipe on the right side. A last-instant dodge past a Bludger is a Perfect Dodge.
BALL FOCUSTap FOCUS and your broom keeps turning toward the play: the Quaffle, the carrier, an open spot for a pass, or the hoop once you have it. Push the stick to override. A gold arrow points the way when it's off screen.
DEFENDYour broom locks onto the carrier and steers you after them. SHOOT becomes STEAL: within about 12 m it launches a homing tackle. Carriers fly slower than everyone else.
FLAIRPerfect dodges, perfect catches, near misses, skimming the grass and slipstreams fill the ring around your crosshair.
THE SNITCHReleased at half time. Tap SEEKER SWAP to chase it. Fly through its golden rings for speed and focus; at half focus GRAB becomes CATCH: hold and swipe for a catch finisher.
FINISHERSAt half energy SHOOT glows gold. Hold it and swipe a direction or draw a circle; keep holding past full charge to let the game pick. Each finisher spends half your energy.
