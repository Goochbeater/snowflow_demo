How to Play

STEERDrag on the left half. Your broom flies forward on its own. Double-tap and hold to air-brake into tight turns.
BOOSTHold the bolt. Tuck into another flyer's slipstream to refill it fast.
PASS & SHOOTPASS throws to the teammate nearest your aim. Hold SHOOT to charge and release to throw at a hoop. Each goal is worth 10.
DODGESwipe on the right side. A last-instant dodge past a Bludger is a Perfect Dodge.
DEFENDWhen they have the Quaffle, SHOOT becomes STEAL. Ram the carrier from behind or the side.
FLAIRPerfect dodges, perfect catches, near misses, skimming the grass and slipstreams fill the ring around your crosshair.
THE SNITCHReleased at half time. Tap SEEKER SWAP to chase it yourself; hold GRAB as you close in, release to snatch it.
FINISHERSWith a full ring and the Quaffle in range, SHOOT glows gold. Hold it and swipe a direction, or draw a circle.
